Welcome to Metervara.

I'm Patrik, a Copenhagen-based designer and developer crafting delightful digital experiences. I'm passionate about real-time visuals, graphics programming and the occasional physics simulation.

I love the fun, the whimsical and the unexpected, and thrive in making interactions feel just right.

Metervara?

Metervara is the Swedish word for items sold by the meter.

I picked the handle up at design school, where it came to reflect a way of thinking about design: the blend between bespoke and mass-produced, between handmade and machine-made.

In practice.

I'm a partner at Manyone, responsible for the development practice and its relationship to design.

Beyond that, I explore ideas in a digital sketchbook, build playful side projects from time to time, and contribute to work culture at our studio.
